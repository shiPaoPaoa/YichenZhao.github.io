### ****Research Assistant** @ Henan Provincial International Joint Laboratory of Intelligent Network Theory and Key Technologies**  
**2023 - 2024**  

During my tenure at the laboratory, I specialized in Distributed Storage and Blockchain. My work involved rigorous academic training, including weekly seminars and extensive reviews of both English and Chinese literature, which honed my skills in independent research and experimental validation.

#### Selected Projects:  
-  **Trusted Carbon Asset Trading Platform** This project focuses on using blockchain to solve trust issues in carbon trading. I designed a system where smart contracts handle automated payments and record proofs of delivery to ensure non-repudiation. A key innovation was the data-splitting verification mechanism (using S1/S2 hashes) and Merkle Tree-based integrity checks. I was responsible for the Smart Contract Architecture and the implementation of core trading algorithms.
- **Medical Rescue Terminal Management Platform** To address the lack of coordination in medical data sharing, I helped build a platform based on HDFS and Spark. By leveraging distributed storage, we achieved high scalability and security for massive medical datasets. I primarily focused on building the distributed system and writing Spark programs for real-time data analysis and multi-dimensional statistics.  
- **Data Analysis on Higher Education Development** In this interdisciplinary project, I analyzed 10 years of inspection data from 40 universities. I developed web crawlers for data acquisition and applied Machine Learning (NLP & PCA) to extract insights from text-heavy reports, providing evidence-based policy recommendations for high-quality university development.
- **Cyber Insurance Roadmap & Security** As cyber-attacks become more frequent, cyber insurance is a critical risk management tool. My research covers four pillars: risk prediction, automated collection, catastrophe modeling, and forensics. I am currently working on predicting security postures using dependency graphs and simulating large-scale ransomware events to help insurers understand systemic risks.
